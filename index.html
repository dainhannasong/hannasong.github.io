I’m a look development artist interested in the little details that make an image feel believable: the softness of a highlight, the warmth in a shadow, the lived-in texture on a surface.

My work moves between shading, lighting, and visual exploration. I love collaborating with a team to find a clear, memorable look that serves the story.
